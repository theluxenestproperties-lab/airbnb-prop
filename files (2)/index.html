Ownership or lease?

We must first establish whether the client owns the apartment or would lease it and operate it as a short-term rental. If leased, explicit written landlord consent for Airbnb or other short-term letting is required, together with confirmation of estate rules and any applicable requirements. We do not recommend proceeding without these permissions.

10 · Risks and feasibility

Assess before any commitment

  • Whether short-term letting is permitted
  • Written landlord approval if leased
  • Estate or body corporate restrictions, including guest access to facilities
  • The actual local booking market for a bachelor unit
  • The advertised R5,300 rent against total operating costs
  • Furnishing investment and payback period
  • Cleaning and turnover logistics
  • Insurance, tax and other applicable obligations

If the financial model does not support the proposal under reasonable assumptions, we will say so. A conservative scenario with rent included may show a thin or negative balance; use the calculator above to test it.
